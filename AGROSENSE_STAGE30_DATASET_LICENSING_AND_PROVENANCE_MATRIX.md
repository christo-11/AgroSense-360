## Status interpretation

- **Verified term** means the cited source documents a term; it does not mean AgroSense-360 is authorized to acquire or use the resource.
- **REQUIRES_REVIEW** means the exact resource, version, rights, or intended use remains unclear.
- **NOT_ACQUIRED** means no dataset snapshot/checksum exists in the source inventory.
- **Stage 23 control:** Project-owner authorization for acquisition and use remains **NO**; pilot research use remains **NOT AUTHORIZED**; ML training, commercial use, and redistribution remain **NOT AUTHORIZED**. These project controls are separate from licence descriptions below.
- Do not infer ML-training permission from public access, an open-data label, or general research permission. The project training gate also requires exact resource/version, permission, provenance, quality/relevance, and signed governance approval.

## Provenance requirements for any future acquisition

Before any dataset is acquired under a separately authorized stage, create a resource-specific record containing:

1. Publisher, exact dataset title, edition/version/run, immutable release date or snapshot ID, official landing page and exact access route.
2. Exact applicable licence and terms version/date; preserve a reviewable copy or official URL snapshot. Record attribution, modifications, commercial use, redistribution/share-alike and third-party terms separately.
3. Explicit intended-use matrix: portfolio/research, pilot, commercial, redistribution, and model training must each have separate outcomes.
4. Project-owner authorization and required source-owner permission evidence; identify who reviewed it and when.
5. Access method and request/asset parameters, retrieval timestamp and timezone, original filename, storage location, file size, SHA-256, and any request/ticket/DOI identifiers.
6. Raw immutable preservation, parser and schema versions, transformation/unit-conversion lineage, quality report, spatial/temporal scope, and any missingness/label limitations.
7. For API products, retain endpoint documentation version, request parameters, response headers/metadata where allowed, and a response checksum; do not place credentials in the manifest.

For sources not acquired, keep version, retrieval date, checksum, record counts, and quality status explicitly unavailable rather than guessing values.
