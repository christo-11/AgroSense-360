# AgroSense-360 Stage 21 Black Pepper Evidence Report

**Pilot:** Black Pepper (`Piper nigrum`)  
**State:** Kerala, India  
**Candidate district:** Idukki (provisional)  
**Purpose:** Research and professional portfolio

## Evidence conclusion

Official sources confirm that ICAR-IISR is a relevant institutional authority for spice research and that it includes an ICAR-IISR Black Pepper application and general crop extension material. However, Stage 21 did not find an exact official Idukki black-pepper publication, district production table, variety/season record or measured soil/disease dataset.

Idukki therefore remains provisional and must not be represented as verified black-pepper cultivation.

## Evidence type assessment

### Measured observations

- No Idukki black-pepper field observations were verified.
- No georeferenced soil lab samples were verified.
- No Idukki IMD station/gauge series was retained and verified.
- No district/crop production record was downloaded or verified.

### Published research findings

- ICAR-IISR institutional research and extension material is an official source for crop practices and guidance.
- Exact black-pepper documents, publication dates and Idukki applicability remain unidentified.
- No exact district-specific peer-reviewed or official evidence was cited here.

### Remote sensing/model estimates

- ERA5-Land is reanalysis data, not direct Idukki observations.
- SoilGrids is a modeled soil product, not a soil lab label.
- NASA GPM and Sentinel-2 are candidate remote-sensing/precipitation or reflectance sources, not verified black-pepper observations.

### Unsupported assumptions

- Idukki is a black-pepper cultivation district.
- A scientific name, variety or growing season is already supported locally.
- Soil properties can be inferred from weather or SoilGrids.
- A crop disease is prevalent in Idukki.
- District production/yield can be calculated from an inaccessible or unverified resource.

None of these is used as a fact in this report.

## Source quality findings

### Stronger official sources

1. ICAR-IISR official crop institute and application listing.
2. Kerala Data Portal official licensing and transparency terms.
3. IMD official public weather service.
4. Copernicus ERA5-Land and Copernicus licence.
5. ISRIC SoilGrids and WoSIS official documentation.

### Weaker or unresolved sources

1. KAU homepage: no exact publication/export or Idukki evidence.
2. data.gov.in: exact resource 403/inaccessible.
3. AGMARKNET: not a direct crop suitability or production label.
4. PlantVillage/PlantDoc: controlled or scraped image datasets, no Idukki evidence.
5. Open-Meteo: model provenance, commercial scope and Idukki validation remain unresolved.

## Agricultural evidence gaps

- No exact Idukki/black-pepper cultivation statement.
- No exact Kerala/Idukki annual area or production.
- No exact variety list or Idukki recommendation.
- No approved planting/harvest season.
- No measured soil pH/N/P/K/texture at black-pepper plots.
- No Idukki station rainfall/temperature series independently compared with observations.
- No expert-confirmed local disease/pest labels.
- No exact publication/license/document version for a number of KAU and Kerala Department documents.

## Claim policy

Until an exact source is retained, the project will state:

- "Idukki is a provisional candidate district."
- "No Idukki/black-pepper cultivation record was found in the reviewed official sources."
- "SoilGrids and ERA5-Land are modeled or estimated covariates, not ground truth."
- "No production, soil or disease observation is available for this pilot."
