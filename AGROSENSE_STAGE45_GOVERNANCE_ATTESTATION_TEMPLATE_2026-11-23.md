## 6. Attestation and authorization boundary

Independent review verifies evidence, completeness, consistency, and process; it does not grant, create, or change action-specific authorization. Owner attestation alone does not grant action-specific authorization. Any permission change requires a separate, valid, explicit, purpose-specific decision and all applicable source-owner rights. No technical readiness, documentation, attestation, or review finding may be interpreted as permission.

The owner attestation, if later completed, records the owner's stated decisions and does not by itself grant permission to act. The supplied owner instruction remains **NO AUTHORIZATION** and is not independently authenticated; effective authorization remains **NOT AUTHORIZED**, and the formal decision-record status remains **PENDING**. A proposed change requires a separate, valid, purpose-specific authorization record and any applicable source-owner permission, licence, or other rights evidence.

This template does not authorize outreach, response handling, dataset acquisition or use, research, ML training, commercial use, redistribution, implementation, integration, or deployment. Technical progress, documentation, public access, or completion of this form does not create authorization.
